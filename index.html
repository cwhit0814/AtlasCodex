Atlas Codex is written from primary sources, not forums or vendor sheets. Where a creator’s view and the published record disagree, the codex follows the record.

Each entry grades its own claims, gives dosing only where a regulator approved it or a trial administered it, and says plainly when nothing is known. It is an educational reference, not medical advice, and not an endorsement of using any compound. The standards and evidence grades are set out in full, and five short guides show how to read a trial and spot a weak claim.
