Tirzepatide

A once-weekly peptide that activates both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors, approved for type 2 diabetes and chronic weight management.

ApprovedRegulatory status
7Controlled trials on file

Dosing

FDA labelEstablished
Type 2 diabetes (Mounjaro)2.5 mg weekly for 4 weeks, then 5 mg; may increase in 2.5 mg steps at 4-week intervals to a maximum of 15 mg
Weight management (Zepbound)Same escalation; maintenance at 5, 10 or 15 mg weekly

Approved and studied amounts, recorded for reference. Not a recommendation, and not adjusted for any individual.

The section's second protocol2 mg/week low, 5 mg/week average, 10-15 mg/week high

The approved maximum on both the Mounjaro and Zepbound labels is 15 mg weekly, reached by escalation, so the top of these ranges meets the label ceiling rather than exceeding it. The two protocols in the section differ at the top by 3 mg a week.

Maximum Pharma Archive: 2.5 mg weekly, titrated to 5, 7.5, 10 or 15 mg weekly, graded as human trial data

Reading this against your own bloodwork

The record above is the same for everyone. What it means for one person depends on their labs, what else they are running, and what they are actually trying to do. That part is a conversation.

Pharmacokinetics

RouteSubcutaneous injection (weekly)
BioavailabilityAbout 80%
Time to peak8-72 hours
Half-lifeAbout 5 days
Steady stateAbout 4 weeks of weekly dosing
MetabolismProteolytic cleavage, β-oxidation of the C20 fatty diacid and amide hydrolysis
ExcretionUrine and feces as metabolites

Monitoring

Fasting glucose and HbA1cThe effect being treated, and the number that shows whether it is working
Weight and waistTracks response, and a rapid fall raises gallstone risk
Lean mass, if availableA meaningful share of weight lost on any large deficit is lean tissue

Interactions

Oral hormonal contraceptivesModerateMay make the pill less reliable after starting or raising the dose
Insulin and sulfonylureasModerateRaises the risk of low blood sugar, including severe episodes
Narrow-margin oral drugs such as warfarinModerateSlowed stomach emptying can delay or blunt absorption of oral drugs
General anesthesia or deep sedationModerateRare reports of food in the stomach being inhaled during anesthesia

Legal status

United StatesFDA-approved and sold by prescription (Mounjaro, Zepbound).
United KingdomLicensed in the UK as Mounjaro; the electronic Medicines Compendium lists it as a prescription only medicine.
European UnionAuthorised EU-wide as Mounjaro since September 2022, and the EMA says it can only be obtained with a prescription.
CanadaTirzepatide is on the Prescription Drug List, and Mounjaro is marketed in Canada.
AustraliaListed in Schedule 4 of the Poisons Standard, so it is a prescription-only medicine.
New ZealandClassified as a prescription medicine in the Medsafe classification database, which follows Schedule 1 of the Medicines Regulations 1984.
JapanListed in Japan's national health insurance drug price standard as a medical-use drug (Mounjaro). Its package insert is marked as a prescription drug (shohosen iyakuhin).
ChinaChina’s drug regulator (NMPA) lists tirzepatide injection (Eli Lilly) with imported-drug approval HJ20240022.
RussiaMunzhelio (tirzepatide) solution for subcutaneous injection, 2.5 to 15 mg, has a record in the State Register of Medicinal Products, marked for dispensing by prescription.
Sport (WADA)Markers of tirzepatide are on the 2026 Monitoring Program, in and out of competition. Tirzepatide itself is not on the Prohibited List.
