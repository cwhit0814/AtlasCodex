Tirzepatide

What it is

A once-weekly peptide that activates both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors, approved for type 2 diabetes and chronic weight management.

C225H348N48O68, molecular weight about 4813.5 g/mol. It is a 39-amino-acid peptide built on the GIP sequence, with Aib residues at positions 2 and 13 and a C20 fatty diacid on lysine 20 for albumin binding. Its half-life is about 5 days.

Gastrointestinal

LowEstablished

The approved maximum on both the Mounjaro and Zepbound labels is 15 mg weekly, reached by escalation, so the top of these ranges meets the label ceiling rather than exceeding it. The two protocols in the section differ at the top by 3 mg a week.

Maximum Pharma Archive gives 2.5 mg weekly, titrated to 5, 7.5, 10 or 15 mg weekly, graded as human trial data

The human record

SURPASS-2: Against semaglutide 1 mg in type 2 diabetes

1,879 adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin. tirzepatide 5, 10 or 15 mg weekly or semaglutide 1 mg weekly. 40 weeks. randomized, open-label, active-controlled

SURMOUNT-1: Weight management without diabetes

2,539 adults, BMI ≥30 or ≥27 with a comorbidity, no diabetes. tirzepatide 5, 10 or 15 mg weekly or placebo. 72 weeks. randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled

SURMOUNT-5: Head to head with semaglutide in obesity

751 adults with obesity, no diabetes. tirzepatide at maximum tolerated dose or semaglutide at maximum tolerated dose. 72 weeks. randomized, open-label

SURPASS-CVOT: Cardiovascular outcomes against dulaglutide

13,165 adults with type 2 diabetes and atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease. tirzepatide or dulaglutide 1.5 mg weekly. event-driven follow-up. randomized, double-blind, active-controlled non-inferiority

SURMOUNT-OSA: Obstructive sleep apnea with obesity

469 adults with moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea and obesity, in two trials (without and with PAP therapy). tirzepatide at maximum tolerated dose (10 or 15 mg weekly) or placebo. 52 weeks. two randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials

SUMMIT: Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction and obesity

731 adults with HFpEF and BMI ≥30. tirzepatide up to 15 mg weekly or placebo. median follow-up 104 weeks. randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled

SURMOUNT-4: What happens when it is stopped

670 adults who had lost a mean 20.9% during a 36-week open-label lead-in. continued tirzepatide 10 or 15 mg weekly or switched to placebo. 52 weeks after randomization. randomized withdrawal, double-blind

What to watch

Fasting glucose and HbA1c
The effect being treated, and the number that shows whether it is working
Weight and waist
Tracks response, and a rapid fall raises gallstone risk
Lean mass, if available
A meaningful share of weight lost on any large deficit is lean tissue

Pharmacokinetics

Route
Subcutaneous injection (weekly)
Bioavailability
About 80%
Time to peak
8-72 hours
Half-life
About 5 days
Steady state
About 4 weeks of weekly dosing
Metabolism
Proteolytic cleavage, β-oxidation of the C20 fatty diacid and amide hydrolysis
Excretion
Urine and feces as metabolites

Legal status

United States
FDA-approved and sold by prescription (Mounjaro, Zepbound).
United Kingdom
Licensed in the UK as Mounjaro; the electronic Medicines Compendium lists it as a prescription only medicine.
European Union
Authorised EU-wide as Mounjaro since September 2022, and the EMA says it can only be obtained with a prescription.
Canada
Tirzepatide is on the Prescription Drug List, and Mounjaro is marketed in Canada.
Australia
Listed in Schedule 4 of the Poisons Standard, so it is a prescription-only medicine.
New Zealand
Classified as a prescription medicine in the Medsafe classification database, which follows Schedule 1 of the Medicines Regulations 1984.
Japan
Listed in Japan's national health insurance drug price standard as a medical-use drug (Mounjaro). Its package insert is marked as a prescription drug (shohosen iyakuhin).
China
China’s drug regulator (NMPA) lists tirzepatide injection (Eli Lilly) with imported-drug approval HJ20240022.
Russia
Munzhelio (tirzepatide) solution for subcutaneous injection, 2.5 to 15 mg, has a record in the State Register of Medicinal Products, marked for dispensing by prescription.
Sport (WADA)
Markers of tirzepatide are on the 2026 Monitoring Program, in and out of competition. Tirzepatide itself is not on the Prohibited List.
