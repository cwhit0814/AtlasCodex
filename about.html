About Atlas Codex

A plain-language atlas of the compounds people are talking about.

What this is

Atlas Codex is a reference library. Each entry covers what a compound is, its structure, how it acts on the body, its risks, and the human clinical record behind it. Every claim carries a grade for the evidence behind it.

Evidence grades

  • human rct Randomized controlled trials in people.
  • established Settled pharmacology.
  • human obs Observational human data.
  • animal in vitro Preclinical only.
  • anecdotal User reports.
  • contested Evidence points both ways.
  • unknown No data.

Status labels

  • approved Approved as a medicine, or as a cosmetic ingredient, in at least one jurisdiction. Each entry says which.
  • investigational In formal clinical development.
  • research only Not approved for human use. Evidence is mostly preclinical.
  • discontinued Was once an approved medicine but has been withdrawn or is no longer marketed.
  • veterinary Approved for animals only.
  • never approved Never approved as a medicine anywhere, for example designer steroids.

Profile scores

Profile bars are editorial judgements on a 1–5 scale, relative to similar compounds. Red rows are attributes where a higher score is worse. Visitors can add their own scores; these are stored only in their own browser.
