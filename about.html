Aboutthe codex

What this is

Atlas Codex is a reference library. Each entry covers what a compound is, its structure, how it acts on the body, its risks, and the human clinical record behind it. Every claim carries a grade for the evidence behind it.

Evidence grades

  • Human RCTRandomized controlled trials in people.
  • EstablishedSettled pharmacology.
  • Human observationalObservational human data.
  • AnimalIn vitroPreclinical only.
  • AnecdotalUser reports.
  • ContestedEvidence points both ways.
  • UnknownNo data.

Status labels

  • ApprovedApproved as a medicine, or as a cosmetic ingredient, in at least one jurisdiction. Each entry says which.
  • InvestigationalIn formal clinical development.
  • Research onlyNot approved for human use. Evidence is mostly preclinical.
  • DiscontinuedWas once an approved medicine but has been withdrawn or is no longer marketed.
  • VeterinaryApproved for animals only.
  • Never approvedNever approved as a medicine anywhere, for example designer steroids.
  • BannedProhibited for human use or unlawful in supplements, for example DNP and DMAA.
  • SupplementSold as a dietary supplement rather than an approved drug.
  • CompoundedMade to prescription by a compounding pharmacy rather than sold as an approved product.
  • CosmeticSold as a cosmetic or personal care product, not as a drug.

Profile scores

Profile bars are editorial judgements on a 1 to 5 scale, relative to similar compounds. Red rows are attributes where a higher score is worse. Visitors can add their own scores; these are stored only in their own browser.
