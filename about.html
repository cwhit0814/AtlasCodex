About Atlas Codex

A plain-language atlas of the compounds people are talking about.

What this is

Atlas Codex is a reference library. Each entry summarises what a compound is, its proposed mechanism, its regulatory status, and the quality of the evidence behind it.

Status labels

  • Approved Approved as a medicine (or, for cosmetics, a permitted ingredient) in at least one jurisdiction. Entries say which.
  • Investigational In formal clinical development.
  • Research only Not approved for human use anywhere relevant. Evidence is mostly preclinical.

Editorial standards

We favour peer-reviewed human data over animal data, and animal data over anecdote. Where evidence is thin, entries say so.

Disclaimer

Atlas Codex is an educational reference. Nothing here is medical advice, and nothing here is an endorsement of using any compound. Talk to a licensed clinician before making health decisions.
